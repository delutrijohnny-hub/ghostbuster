Text Messaging Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

Market Maker Management sends text messages about appointments that people have booked with us. This page explains who gets messaged, what the messages are, and how to stop them.

Who we text

We text people who have booked a strategy call or consultation with us and provided their mobile number as part of that booking. The booking form states that we will send text messages about the appointment. We do not buy, rent, or import contact lists, and we do not message anyone who has not booked with us.

What we send

Confirmations of the appointment, reminders before it, and follow-ups afterwards. Messages relate only to the appointment that person booked. Typical examples:

  • “Hi Sarah, Johnny here. We're set for Oct 15 at 6:08 PM PDT. If there's something in particular you want covered, reply and I'll make sure we get to it. Reply STOP to opt out.”
  • “Hey Sarah, quick heads up that we're on for this Thursday at 6:08 PM PDT. Nothing needed from you beforehand - just keep it on the calendar.”
  • “Hey Sarah, we're on in about an hour at 6:08 PM PDT. Here's the link so it's handy: https://meet.google.com/abc-defg-hij”

Message frequency varies with your appointment — typically a handful of messages around the time of a booked call. Message and data rates may apply.

How to stop

Reply STOP to any message and we will not text you again. We also honour STOPALL, UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, END, QUIT, REVOKE and OPTOUT. The opt-out is recorded immediately and automatically; no one has to action it by hand, and it applies to every number on your record.

Reply START or UNSTOP to begin receiving messages again.

Help

Reply HELP to any message, or email john@marketmakermgmt.com.

When we send

Only between 8am and 9pm in your own local time. This is enforced automatically by the system that sends the messages, not left to whoever is sending.

Your information

We use your mobile number to send messages about your appointment and nothing else. We do not sell, rent or share mobile numbers or messaging consent with anyone, and no mobile information is shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing purposes. Numbers are passed only to the messaging provider that delivers the message on our behalf.

See also our Privacy Policy.
