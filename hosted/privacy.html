GhostBuster — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 16, 2026

GhostBuster is a private scheduling and follow-up tool built for Market Maker Management (John DeLutri) to manage YouTube coaching call bookings and client communication. It is not offered publicly as a product — this policy explains what it does with data from the single connected Google account.

Information we access

  • Google Calendar (read-only). Event times, titles, and attendee names/emails for events on the connected calendar, used only to detect newly booked coaching calls and keep the client list in sync.
  • Google account profile. Your email address, used to identify your account when you sign in.

How information is used

  • To automatically detect new bookings from the connected calendar and create or update the matching client record.
  • To prepare follow-up text messages about scheduled calls, opened in the account holder's own Messages app to send.
  • Optionally, when the account holder manually clicks "Generate with AI," a specific client's notes and call context (not calendar data) are sent to Google's Gemini API to draft a personalized message. This only happens on manual request.

Data storage

Client and scheduling data is stored in a private Supabase (PostgreSQL) database, accessible only to the authenticated account holder. Google Calendar refresh and access tokens are stored server-side and are never exposed to the browser.

Data sharing

We do not sell or share personal data with third parties. Data is sent only to Google (Calendar API, for sync) and, only when manually triggered, to Google's Gemini API (for the AI message-drafting feature described above) — solely to provide that functionality.

Data retention & deletion

Data is retained as long as the account is active. To request deletion of data or revoke calendar access, contact john@marketmakermgmt.com, or remove GhostBuster's access directly from your Google Account's Third-party access settings.
