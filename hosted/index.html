What it does

Four questions, answered every morning

Your salespeople shouldn't open a CRM and be handed a database. They should be handed a list.

01

Who?

Every contact gets a Ghost Score from 0–100, ranked so the top of the list is where the day starts.

02

When?

A follow-up cadence that runs itself — and pauses the moment someone writes back, so nobody gets blasted mid-conversation.

03

How?

Call or text, decided per person. After enough unanswered texts, Ghost Recall tells you to pick up the phone instead.

04

What?

A written message, ready to send, and Ghost Recall learns which version actually produces appointments.
