Market Maker Management sends text messages about appointments that people
have booked with us. This page explains who we message, what we send, and how
to stop.

## Who we text

We text people who have booked a strategy call or consultation with us and
who provided their mobile number as part of that booking. The booking page
states that by booking, they agree to receive text messages about the
appointment.

We do not buy, rent, or import contact lists, and we do not message anyone
who has not booked with us.

## What we send

Confirmations of the appointment, reminders before it, and follow-ups
afterwards. Messages relate only to the appointment that person booked.

Examples of the messages we send:

- "Hi Sarah, Johnny here. We're set for Oct 15 at 6:08 PM PDT. If there's
  something in particular you want covered, reply and I'll make sure we get
  to it. Reply STOP to opt out."
- "Hey Sarah, quick heads up that we're on for this Thursday at 6:08 PM PDT.
  Nothing needed from you beforehand - just keep it on the calendar."
- "Hey Sarah, we're on in about an hour at 6:08 PM PDT. Here's the link so
  it's handy: https://meet.google.com/abc-defg-hij"

Message frequency varies with your appointment, typically a handful of
messages around the time of a booked call. Message and data rates may apply.

## How to stop

Reply STOP to any message and we will not text you again. We also honour
STOPALL, UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, END, QUIT, REVOKE and OPTOUT. The opt-out is
recorded immediately and automatically, and applies to every record holding
that number.

Reply START or UNSTOP to begin receiving messages again.

## Help

Reply HELP to any message, or email john@marketmakermgmt.com.

## When we send

Only between 8am and 9pm in your own local time.

## Your information

We use your mobile number to send messages about your appointment and nothing
else. We do not sell, rent or share mobile numbers or messaging consent with
anyone, and no mobile information is shared with third parties or affiliates
for marketing purposes. Numbers are passed only to the messaging provider
that delivers the message on our behalf.

See also our Privacy Policy.

WHY EACH PART IS THERE
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
- The no-sharing sentence is the single line reviewers look for by name, and
  it has to appear in the privacy policy, not only on a separate page.
- The sample messages must match what actually goes out. These are produced
  by the live templates; if the copy changes materially, change them here.
- The opt-out keyword list matches what the system actually honours
  (smsKeyword in logic.js). Do not list a keyword the system ignores.
- The 8am-9pm line matches the quiet-hours rule enforced in send-sms.
